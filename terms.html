1. What PWAfy does and doesn't do

PWAfy generates PWA-related files client-side, in your browser. It does not host, mirror, or rehost your website, and it does not modify your live site. You're responsible for copying the generated files onto your own server or hosting provider.

The optional "scan a URL" feature fetches the public HTML of a page you provide to pre-fill the builder. This request is proxied through our server so we can read response headers your browser otherwise couldn't; we do not store the fetched page content beyond a short cache used to speed up repeat scans of the same URL.
