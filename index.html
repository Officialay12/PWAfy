What one click actually produces

PWAfy is honest about what a browser can and can't do for you. It won't rehost your website no tool can promise that safely. What it does do is generate every file a browser needs to treat your site as an installable app, correctly and to spec, then check its own work.

Identity files
A manifest.json validated against the W3C spec, plus the head tags and iOS-specific meta tags most converters forget.
A full icon set
One image in, eight sizes out, properly padded maskable versions, favicon.ico, and the exact iOS splash screens Apple's engine expects.
A quality score
Every build is checked against the same criteria installability engines use, with a plain-language fix list for anything that's missing.
