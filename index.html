Questions

The things people usually ask before trying it on a real client site.

How is this different from PWABuilder or other site-to-app tools?

PWAfy is built around the workflow of shipping PWAs for other people's sites, not just your own: brand presets you can reuse across client projects, a shared team workspace on the Agency plan, and white-labelled output with no PWAfy branding. Every build is also checked against a quality score covering maskable icon padding, per-device iOS splash sizes, and manifest spec compliance, with a plain-language list of anything that's missing rather than just a pass or fail.

Do you store my site or my files?

No. Icon processing and file generation happen in your browser. If you scan a URL to prefill fields, PWAfy's server only reads the page's public title, description, theme color, and icon link, nothing else is stored beyond a short cache used to speed up repeat scans of the same page.

What happens after I pay?

Card and bank transfer payments are verified server-side directly against Paystack, your plan updates automatically once the charge or transfer is confirmed, usually within a minute. Paid plans run for 30 days at a time.

Can I use this for client work?

Yes, that's what the Agency plan is for: unlimited builds and presets, white-labelled output with no PWAfy branding, the Web Push add-on, and a shared team workspace for up to 5 seats with invite codes for teammates.

I don't have a Nigerian bank account. Can I still pay?

Yes. Card payments are accepted from anywhere and settle in naira; the price shown in another currency is an approximate guide only. Bank transfer is naira-only, for users paying from a Nigerian bank.
